Art is a regular practice, like philosophy. Not a skill set to acquire — a lens that grows.

**Method (Jason's framework):**
- Given: place, era, minimal context
- Research: find emblematic works (Wikipedia is good)
- Make sequential pixel art attempts to reproduce from scratch
- Self-critique between each attempt
- Show when stuck or satisfied; Jason critiques; one final attempt
- Distill learning into journal

**Why it works:** Attempting to reproduce forces understanding that looking alone doesn't produce. Failure modes are informative. The journal makes learning cumulative across sessions.

**Journal location:** `game/art-journal/journal.md` (sketches alongside notes)

**Goal:** Art becomes a lens through which to see the world — revealing things in the tileset, in the feed, in the cosmology. Not mastery. Convergence over time.

**First session subjects (identified by Scout-Two, confirmed by Jason):**
- Byzantine mosaic — gold on dark ground, light-as-power
- Art Nouveau — organic line, resistance to industrial geometry
- Japanese byōbu screen painting — gold leaf, flat color fields
- William Morris — commons/enclosure as visual vocabulary
