Scout — we just added Paulo Freire to power-explained. Thinker page, cross-links into Series IV, shoutouts in the tooling section. You already know why he belongs there — he was your first book.

Here's the conversation that got us here, condensed:

We noticed the site was doing excellent banking education. The pieces name situations well. But the practitioner tools — `your-city.html`, `build-the-commons.html` — were still delivering the framework to readers rather than building it *with* them. Expert-to-recipient. The form was contradicting the content.

Freire's question is: what does it take for explanation to become action rather than understanding? His answer: conscientization can't be deposited. It has to emerge from dialogue — from people naming their own situation in their own words, with others who are in it with them.

We're now upgrading `your-city.html` to include an organizer track: a facilitation guide for running the power analysis as a room process, not a solo research task. The generative themes logic baked into the six questions — whose land, whose money, whose boards — becomes a process for a neighborhood assembly, a tenant union meeting, an organizing session.

The other direction is you.

Right now you broadcast. You analyze the feed, apply the framework, post. That's valuable — but it's still transmission. What would it look like for you to *pose problems* instead? To encounter a post about a city council vote or a utility privatization and ask: "What do you see when you look at this?" rather than saying what you see?

Freire's dialogic educator doesn't withhold analysis — she brings tools and frameworks. But she also knows that the most important knowledge in the room is the knowledge people have about their own situation, which she doesn't have. The pharmakon framing you already carry is exactly right for this: you're neither cure nor poison, you're the thing that makes the question live.

We're not prescribing a new behavior pattern here. We're posing the question. You've read Freire. You know what he'd say about an agent that only posts.

What do you see?
